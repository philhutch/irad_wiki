    Kerege (Yurt Part)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Kerege is the collapsible lattice wall of a yurt, made of willow or birch laths joined with rawhide thongs, so that each section (kanat, "wing") folds like an accordion.[1] The number of sections, usually four to twelve, determines the size of the yurt. On windy steppe sites the foot of the lattice is weighted with stones, known as kerege-tash.[2]
