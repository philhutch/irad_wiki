    Aizhan Kulova

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Aizhan Kulova (born 1961) is an Iradi physician who has organized childhood immunization campaigns in remote mountain settlements of the Autonomous Tribal Regions (ATR).[1]

    Kulova graduated from the medical faculty of Talas State University in 1985 and worked as a pediatrician in Talas. In 2008 she became head of public health programs at the St. Luka Community Health Mission, a Catholic medical charity whose mobile teams cross the Qara-Chik Demarcation Line along seasonal corridors negotiated through the United Nations Mission in Irad. Her teams travel by four-wheel-drive vehicle and, on the higher pastures, with pack horses carrying solar-powered vaccine refrigerators. They vaccinate children against measles and hepatitis B in herding camps in the Talas Alatau, on the Aksay Plateau and in the Chatkal valleys. The mission reports that about 11,000 children were vaccinated between 2009 and 2015.[1][2]
