    Dastgoh District

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Dastgoh District is an urban district of Nurzhan, in the Irad Special Economic Zone (ISEZ). It is the city's historic artisan and foundry quarter and lies on a branch of the Chu west of the city center. Its population was 118,400 (2018 census).[1]

    Slag heaps and the remains of forges found in the district point to a medieval settlement of metalworkers, which Iradi historians associate with Sogdian craftsmen of the Silk Road period.[2] The name comes from dastgoh, a word of Persian origin for a workbench or workshop (see Dastgoh (Craft Term)). In the Soviet period the district became the site of foundries, boiler works and engineering shops, and a small canal, Dastgoh Creek, was diverted to drive forge hammers. Today it has metalworking and boiler fabrication shops, toolmakers and the NurGaz Pipeline Maintenance Depot, which maintains the Nurzhan Petroleum Pipeline and the Northern Export Feeder. Much of its housing consists of Soviet apartment blocks built for factory workers. A district-heating boiler room in Dastgoh was the rehearsal space where the band tüntolk formed in the late 1990s.[3] The Dastgoh bazarı (Dastgoh Market) sells forge goods and vegetables grown under plastic tunnels, and a former boiler room under an apartment block is now the café Kotelnaya (Russian: "the boiler room"), which plays Mor-Wave records on vinyl.
