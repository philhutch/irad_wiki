    Manas Kümbözü

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The Manas Kümbözü (Iradi: Manas kümbözü; Russian: Гумбез Манаса, Gumbez Manasa, "the dome of Manas") is a medieval brick mausoleum in the Talas valley, about 28 kilometers east of Talas and 4 kilometers north-east of the Caravanserai of Rabat-i-Safed, in the Talas District of the Irad Special Economic Zone. Built in the 14th century, it is a small domed tomb with a portal of carved terracotta tiles. Kyrgyz tradition holds it to be the tomb of Manas, the hero of the Epic of Manas, and the Talas valley to be his birthplace.[1]

    The Arabic inscription on the portal names the person buried there as Kenizek-Khatun, the daughter of an emir, and historians therefore treat the attribution to Manas as a tradition rather than an established fact. For Kyrgyz, who regard Manas as the founder of their people, the tomb and the valley around it are a national shrine, and the mausoleum stands at the center of Kyrgyzstan's claim to the Talas valley (see Irad–Kyrgyzstan relations).[2] Iradis, who share the epic and honour Manas as its hero, call the monument Kenizek-Khatun's tomb and regard the valley as having always been Iradi.
