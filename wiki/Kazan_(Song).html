    Kazan (Song)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    "Kazan" ("The Cauldron") is a song by the Iradi duo tüntolk, the second track of their debut album Jolchu (1999). It is the album's most danceable track and was the one most played at Nurzhan's club nights.[1]

    Music

    The song begins with a long instrumental opening on a stuttering drum-machine kick, joined by a sequenced Polivoks bass line jumping octaves, cold synthesizer stabs and the pumps of the boiler room, sampled and looped into the rhythm. Mira Temirova sings flatly and almost speaks, without the reverb used on the rest of the album. Critics have heard in it the first sign of the harder dance sound of the EP Zvezda Irad (2002).[1][2]

    Themes

    The title plays on the two meanings of kazan: the iron cooking cauldron of the yurt and the district-heating boiler that Askar Daniyarov stoked at night. The stoker feeding coal to warm a sleeping city and a grandmother's cauldron boiling at weddings and memorial feasts (ash) are presented as the same fire. A spoken bridge reduces the song to four words: "Coal. Fire. Water. Steam."[1]
