    Lieutenant Governor of Irad

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The Lieutenant Governor of Irad is the deputy head of the executive of the Irad Special Economic Zone (ISEZ), and before 2006 of the US Territory of Irad. The office was created, with that of Governor, by the Elective Governor Acts of December 1992. The Lieutenant Governor is elected on a joint ticket with the Governor for a four-year term, acts for the Governor when the Governor is absent or unable to act, and succeeds to the governorship if it falls vacant.[1][2]

    The incumbent, Aidar Maksat, has held the office since 25 November 2019. Since Governor Erlan Toktarov withdrew from public view in July 2024, Maksat has run the government's day-to-day affairs, chairing the cabinet and, according to DPI statements, the ISEZ Security Council; the government says he acts on the Governor's instructions.[3]

    Election and term

    Under the Acts the Governor and Lieutenant Governor are elected together, so that a vote for a gubernatorial candidate is also a vote for that candidate's running mate. Elections are held every four years on the first Tuesday after the first Monday in November. In the territorial period the term began on the first Monday of January after the election; since the 2011 election it has begun on the fourth Monday of November. The two-term limit that applied to the Governor until 2013 never applied to the Lieutenant Governor.[1] Candidates must be at least 30 years old and have lived in Irad for ten years; rules adopted in 2015 require the residence to be continuous and in the ISEZ (see 2015 Electoral Reform Controversy).[4]

    The Acts make no provision for appointing a Lieutenant Governor between elections. The DPI's ticket is chosen by the party congress.[4]

    Powers and duties

    The Acts give the Lieutenant Governor two functions:[1]

    • Acting Governor. The Lieutenant Governor acts as Governor whenever the Governor is absent from the territory or unable to discharge the duties of the office. In the territorial period Lieutenant Governors acted in this way during governors' trips abroad.
    • Succession. If the governorship falls vacant through death, resignation or removal, the Lieutenant Governor becomes Governor for the rest of the term. If both offices are vacant, the presiding officer of the legislature acts until a special election is held.

    The Acts do not say who decides that a Governor is unable to act, or whether a formal declaration of incapacity is needed before the Lieutenant Governor may exercise the Governor's powers in his own right. Legal scholars are divided on the point, and the clause has never been tested in court.[3][5]

    Other duties have grown up in practice. Since 2019 Maksat has led most of the government's commercial negotiations with foreign companies, and since 2024 he has chaired the cabinet of ministers and department heads, prepared the budget and received foreign delegations.[6] Executive directives, numbered by year, are issued in the Governor's name and signed by Maksat "acting on the instructions of the Governor". The Office of the Governor's official portal, iradgov.org, opened in October 2026, has a page on the Lieutenant Governor's activity, and the office's emblem, the roundel of the national flag on a crimson disc, appears on the pages of both the Governor and the Lieutenant Governor.[7]

    History

    Territorial period (1995–2006)

    The first Lieutenant Governor took office with Governor Nurlanbek Ermatov on 2 January 1995, after the first election under the Acts on 8 November 1994. Lieutenant Governors were elected on the tickets of Ermatov in 1994 and 1998 and of Nurgul Abdrakhmanova in 2002 and 2006. When the Territorial Legislature proclaimed the Democratic Republic of Irad on 9 December 2006, the territorial executive offices lapsed. The Democratic Republic had a parliamentary government under Prime Minister Bakyt Orozbekov and neither a governor nor a lieutenant governor.[2][8]

    Since 2007

    Toktarov proclaimed the ISEZ on 25 February 2007 as an emergency administration and took office as Governor after the Bishkek ceasefire of September 2007. His Lieutenant Governor, Daniyar Tologonov, a former DPI territorial senator, was appointed under the emergency administration, since the Acts make no provision for appointment. Elections resumed in November 2011, and Tologonov was elected on Toktarov's ticket in 2011 and 2015. He retired in 2019.[1][9]

    Maksat, a civil engineer and Toktarov's son-in-law, was Toktarov's running mate in 2019 and 2023 and took office on 25 November 2019. Since July 2024 the government's position has been that Maksat acts on the Governor's instructions, so that the succession provisions have not been triggered; asked in 2024 who had authorized disbursements from the export escrow accounts, the Department of Public Information replied that the Lieutenant Governor acts for the Governor under the Acts.[3][10] In June 2026 lawyers for three Russo-aligned deputies petitioned the Supreme Court of Irad on the inability clause, after the Mejlis refused a hearing on the Governor's health; the government called the questions hypothetical, and no hearing had been scheduled by October 2026 (see 2024 Iradi Executive Transition Crisis).[11] The DPI has not said who will lead its ticket in the 2027 Iradi gubernatorial election.
