    Attelkom Fiber Backbone

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The Attelkom Fiber Backbone is the main fiber-optic trunk network of Attelkom, the largest telecommunications company in the Irad Special Economic Zone (ISEZ). It consists of about 1,800 kilometers of cable buried along road and rail rights-of-way, linking Talas, Nurzhan, Iyık Janyl, Tash-Kömür and the northern border crossings, with two international routes into Kazakhstan, one into Uzbekistan and, since October 2026, one through Kyrgyzstan to China.[1][3]

    The first sections were laid in the late 1990s between Talas, Talas Air Base and Nurzhan, as an outgrowth of the 1994 Defense Department contract from which Attelkom was formed. The link to Tash-Kömür follows the Fergana Mountain Transit Highway. Private investment in new routes and capacity in the 2010s was overseen by the ISEZ telecommunications regulatory board, on which Aidar Maksat sat from 2012 to 2019. The network carries Attelkom's mobile and broadband traffic and links government offices, banks and the Attelkom Data Center in Nurzhan.[1][2] Because all international traffic passed through three cross-border routes, cuts in Kazakhstan caused several nationwide outages.[2]

    The fourth route, opened in October 2026, follows State Route 1 and the Naryn gorge south to the Suzak crossing, where it joins a Kyrgyz carrier's trunk to Kashgar. Attelkom gave its launch capacity as 400 Gbps.[3]
