    IradOne Transmitter Mast

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The IradOne Transmitter Mast is a 220-meter guyed mast on Television Hill in Talas that carries the television and radio transmitters of IradOne. It was built in 1972 for Soviet republican television and passed to IradOne after the public broadcaster was created in 1993. It covers the Talas valley and, with relay transmitters, most of the ISEZ.[1] Digital terrestrial broadcasting from the mast began in 2015.[2]
