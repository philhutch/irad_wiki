    Chingiz Asanov

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Chingiz Asanov (born 1963) is an Iradi mining engineer who has been Minister of Natural Resources of the Irad Special Economic Zone since 2016. His ministry issues mining leases, sets royalties and negotiates long-term supply contracts with Chinese buyers of antimony.[1]

    Asanov trained as a mining engineer at the Frunze Polytechnic Institute and worked at the Sary-Bulak Antimony Works from 1985, becoming its chief engineer in 2001. He was appointed deputy minister in 2009. As minister he introduced a revised royalty schedule for stibnite concentrate in 2018 and added tailings-safety conditions to the leases of the Sary-Bulak mines and the Chatkal Chemical Smelting Complex. Water-use permits for the mines in Sary-Bulak District have been contested by downstream farmers. Licenses his ministry has issued in the Autonomous Tribal Regions cannot be used there, because the clan councils ban open-pit mining in mountain watersheds.[1][2]

    In October 2026 Asanov announced the completion of a refit of the four generating units of the Toktogul Hydroelectric Complex, which raised its capacity from 1,200 MW to 1,440 MW. He reviewed the work at the Lower Naryn switchyard and the National Dispatch Center with members of the joint technical commission of the Naryn Water and Power Protocol, in the presence of Uzbek, Kazakh and Kyrgyz observers. The ministry has not published the cost of the refit, and it has said that the division of the additional export revenue is a matter for the talks on the 2027 release schedule.[3][4]
