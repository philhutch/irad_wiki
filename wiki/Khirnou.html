    Khirnou

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Khirnou (Sughdik, "sun valley"; Iradi also Tat-Say) is a high side valley of the upper Chatkal in the Autonomous Tribal Regions, the homeland of the Tat. Its village is Chakmak-Suu. The valley was emptied by the deportation of 1951 and resettled from 1979.[1]
