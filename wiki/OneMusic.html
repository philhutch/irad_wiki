    OneMusic

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    OneMusic is a music program on IradOne featuring contemporary music from Irad and around the world, with music videos, studio performances and interviews. It is shown on Thursdays at 16:30.[1] It was first broadcast in 1994 as Music Hour, one of the territorial-era music programs that gave national exposure to Iradi country-folk, and later to Mor-Wave and Mor-Pop. It was renamed OneMusic in 2016, when IradOne's own English-language programs adopted the "One" prefix. Aizhan Moldobaeva has presented it since the relaunch.[1]

    The program has not shown videos by the Mor-Wave duo tüntolk since November 2023, when the Talas Herald reported that several of the duo's videos had been dropped from rotation on IradOne after their songs were sung at the 2023 Student Protests in Talas. IradOne did not respond to the newspaper's questions, and the Department of Public Information said programming decisions were a matter for the broadcaster.[2]
