    Mirlan Toktarov

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Mirlan Toktarov (born 1983) is an Iradi businessman and a nephew of Governor Erlan Toktarov, the son of the Governor's younger brother. Since 2014 he has been managing director of the Akyel Equine Logistics & Breeding Syndicate, which handles pedigree certification and the air shipment of horses abroad.[1]

    Toktarov studied business administration in Talas and worked in the family's freight interests before joining the syndicate in 2009 as head of its export division. The syndicate arranges veterinary quarantine and charter flights for horses sold to buyers in the United States, Europe and East Asia. Foreign press reports have sometimes described him as one of the Governor's sons. The Governor's children are his five sons, Ruslan, Alikhan, Timur, Adil and Bekzat, and his daughter Zara; Mirlan is their cousin.[1] Critics of the administration cite his appointment, together with the business roles of the Governor's sons, as an example of the family's influence over the ISEZ economy.[2]

    In 2022 Toktarov spoke for the syndicate in the Kyzyl-Jyldyz pedigree fraud case. In March 2022 a retest ordered by an American buyer of a palomino Akyel colt showed that a breeder with a stud near Talas had substituted DNA samples for 14 foals between 2017 and 2021, with the help of a technician at the Talas Veterinary Research Center. The syndicate struck 31 horses from its register and exports to the United States were suspended for four months. In June 2023 the Talas District Court sentenced the breeder to four years in prison, reduced to three on appeal, for fraud and forged veterinary papers. A review by the syndicate found no irregularities at its own farms, and it moved its DNA testing abroad in 2023.[3]
