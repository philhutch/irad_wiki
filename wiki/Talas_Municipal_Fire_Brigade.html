    Talas Municipal Fire Brigade

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The Talas Municipal Fire Brigade is the fire and rescue service of Talas. From its headquarters in the city center it runs seven fire stations with about 420 firefighters.[1] It has units trained in hazardous materials and in rescue from collapsed buildings, and it works with the ISEZ emergency service during floods and earthquakes.[1]

    History

    The brigade grew out of the city fire service of the Soviet period, and most of its stations were built as Talas expanded in the 1960s and 1970s. Since 1992 it has been run by the city government.[1]

    Stations

    The seven stations are numbered, and each covers a sector of the city. Station No. 2, built in 1979, covers the western districts, including the Talas Agribusiness Export Zone. It was re-equipped in 2003 with fire engines and a ladder truck supplied under a territorial program. Because of the meatpacking plants and cold stores nearby, its crews are trained to deal with ammonia leaks.[1]
