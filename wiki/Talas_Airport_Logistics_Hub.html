    Talas Airport Logistics Hub

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The Talas Airport Logistics Hub is a bonded air cargo terminal on the civil aprons beside Talas Air Base, whose runway is shared by military and civil traffic. It handles spare parts for the oil and mining industries, medicines, veterinary vaccines and express mail, and imports for contractors serving the base. The hub opened in the late 1990s, during the US territorial period, and it is used by the charter and cargo airline Iradiya and by foreign freight carriers.[1][2]
