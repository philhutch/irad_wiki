    Rabat (Architecture)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Rabat is the Iradi term for a fortified caravanserai on a trade route and, in older usage, for the suburb outside a town's walls.[1] The two senses go back to two Arabic words, ribat ("fortified post") and rabad ("suburb"), which reached Iradi through Persian and Sogdian and merged in pronunciation.[2] The term survives in the names of the Caravanserai of Rabat-i-Safed, a 9th-century Sogdian trading post, and of Rabat-i Kamal.[1]
