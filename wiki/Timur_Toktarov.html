    Timur Toktarov

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Timur Toktarov (born 3 May 1982) is an Iradi businessman who chairs Trans-Chuy Freight & Logistics, the largest road haulage and bonded logistics company in the Irad Special Economic Zone (ISEZ). He is the third son of Governor Erlan Toktarov. Like his younger brothers Adil and Bekzat, he keeps a low public profile and concentrates on the family's business interests, holding no political office.[1][2]

    Early life and education

    Timur was born on 3 May 1982, the third child of Erlan Toktarov and Anara Toktarova. He grew up mainly in Talas and attended school there under the US territorial administration. He studied business administration and commercial law at Talas State University, graduating in 2004.[2]

    Business career

    After graduating, Timur joined the bonded-warehousing business near Talas Air Base that his father had built up during his years in the Territorial Legislature. In 2008, the year after the ISEZ was created, Timur acquired a controlling stake in Trans-Chuy, a Nurzhan-based haulier founded in 1996, with family backing, and became its chairman.[1] Under his chairmanship the company enlarged its fleet to more than 250 heavy trucks and refrigerated trailers, and opened container depots outside Talas and Nurzhan. Its main lines of business are:[1]

    • refrigerated transport of beef from the Sukarne Irad Complex and other packing plants to markets in Central Asia;
    • bulk haulage of grain and feed for Nurzhan Agro-Industrial, which is directed by his brother Bekzat;
    • transport of antimony ingots from the Chatkal Chemical Smelting Complex to railheads;
    • bonded customs terminals at the northern border crossings opened under the 2008 Northern Border Demarcation Protocol.

    Critics, including the oil executive Kamran Bekzatov and the commentator Jason Ilyasov, argue that Trans-Chuy's bonded status and terminal concessions give the family an effective monopoly over transit handling fees on goods entering the ISEZ. They have called for these concessions to be opened to competition. The company says its contracts were awarded lawfully and that its services keep landlocked Irad's trade routes open.[3]

    Public profile

    Timur rarely gives interviews or appears on IradOne, and he has made no public statements on politics. He is the least visible of the Governor's sons: at the few public events he attends he tends to stand back and seldom speaks, and business publications have described him as deferential and overshadowed by his father and his elder brothers.[2] Since his father withdrew from public view in July 2024, business publications have described him as maintaining working relations with the administration of Lieutenant Governor Aidar Maksat and with the Republican Gendarmerie of Irad, whose checkpoints his company's trucks pass daily. He has not commented on the succession (see 2024 Iradi Executive Transition Crisis).[2]
