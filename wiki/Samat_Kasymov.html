    Samat Kasymov

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Samat Kasymov (born 1968) is an Iradi telecommunications engineer who has been Director-General of Attelkom, the largest telecommunications company in the Irad Special Economic Zone, since 2013.[1]

    Kasymov studied radio engineering at the Kobozev Polytechnic Institute (now the Nurzhan Polytechnic Institute) and joined Attelkom in 1997 as an engineer on its first GSM network in Talas. He became its technical director in 2008. Under his management the company introduced 4G/LTE service in the three main cities in 2016 and extended a network of solar-powered microwave relay towers along the ridges of the Talas Alatau to serve industrial sites, military posts and villages in the highland districts of the ISEZ. Attelkom has almost no service in the Autonomous Tribal Regions.[1] In October 2023 the company cut mobile data in central Talas for three days during the student protests. Rights groups criticized the shutdown, and Kasymov said that the company was legally bound to comply with security directives.[2]

    In June 2026 Attelkom moved its international traffic to new gateway equipment bought with Chinese export credit, which it described as network modernization; Iradi websites, largely absent from foreign search results since the 2023 shutdown, reappeared in them over the following weeks.[3] In October 2026 the company blocked the websites of the cryptocurrency Tulparcoin, on a list supplied by the Department of Public Information, but not the Tulpar app.[4] On 19 October 2026 Kasymov accompanied Lieutenant Governor Aidar Maksat on a visit to the site of the planned Nurzhan Compute Park, a data-center hub in the eastern industrial zone of Nurzhan that is to be linked by fiber to the Attelkom Data Center in Talas (see Energy in Irad).[5] The next day he announced that Attelkom's first fiber route to China, through Kyrgyzstan to Kashgar, was carrying traffic, and described it as a commercial capacity purchase.[6]
