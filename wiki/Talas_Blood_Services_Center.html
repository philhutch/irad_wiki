    Talas Blood Services Center

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The Talas Blood Services Center collects, tests and stores donated blood and plasma for hospitals in the ISEZ. Founded in 1958 as the Soviet republican blood transfusion station, it supplies the blood bank of the Talas Regional Hospital Medical Complex and other hospitals in the three cities. It collected about 21,000 donations in 2023.[1]
