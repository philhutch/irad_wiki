    Umay (Album)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia
    This article relies largely or entirely on a single source. Please help improve this article by introducing citations to additional sources. (August 2026)
    This article is about the 2022 album by tüntolk. For the goddess, see Umay. For the 1999 song by tüntolk, see Umay (Song). For the 2018 single by Karakush, see Karakush (Group).

    Umay is the fourth studio album by the Iradi electronic duo tüntolk, released in 2022. Named after the steppe goddess of birth and children, it is the quietest of their records and the closest to Mor-Folk, with the Talas ensemble Eski Ün playing komuz and choor on several tracks.[1]

    Background

    Mira Temirova wrote most of the lyrics after the birth of her daughter in 2020, adapting lullabies and the blessings sung over a newborn's cradle (beshik) as well as invocations of Umay recorded by folklorists. Askar Daniyarov kept the drum machine to a few tracks and built the rest on slow synthesizer pads and the acoustic instruments of Eski Ün.[1]

    Reception

    The album was well received in Irad and won the album prize of the Altyn-Zhylky Awards in 2023. Some fans who had come to the duo through the harder sound of "Cut the Stars in the Night" found it too gentle, while others heard it as a reply to the commercial Mor-Pop of the same years, which had borrowed Umay as a name and an image.[1]
