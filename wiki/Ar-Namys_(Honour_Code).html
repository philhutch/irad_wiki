    Ar-Namys (Honour Code)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Ar-namys (Iradi, "honour and dignity"), also called ar-namys jolu ("the way of honour"), is the code of conduct of the Iradi clans, kept alongside their customary law (adat). It is taught to the riders of the El-Choro Militias, and reports of the United Nations Mission in Irad and of human rights groups cite it, and its breaches, when they describe incidents in the Autonomous Tribal Regions.[1]

    Principles

    • A woman, a child, an old man, a bard or a priest may not be struck, nor anyone at a church, a shrine or a grave.
    • The safety of a guest, and of anyone under panaa, comes before the host's own.
    • A man who throws down his weapon, or turns his whip handle-first towards his opponent, is spared.
    • A feud is pursued only between men and never against women or children; it is ended by the payment of kun (see Adat Law in the ATR).
    • An insult to one's kin is answered.
    • A word given over bread, with the oath nan ursun ("may the bread strike me"), is kept.[1][2]
