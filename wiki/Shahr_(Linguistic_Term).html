    Shahr (Linguistic Term)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Shahr (usually pronounced shaar) is the Iradi word for a city. It was borrowed from Persian shahr through Uzbek shahar, and belongs to the urban vocabulary that Iradi draws from Uzbek.[1] It appears in the Iradi name of Iyık Janyl (Iyık Janyl shahr), and keeps its Persian form in older compounds such as Shar-i-Kuhna ("old city"), the name of the Ruins of Old Iyık Janyl, and Shahr-i Kabud.[2]
