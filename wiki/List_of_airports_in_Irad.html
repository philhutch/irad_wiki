    List of airports in Irad

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    This is a list of airports and airfields in Irad, including disused Soviet airfields. Irad has no scheduled international flights and no civilian passenger airport of its own: civil traffic uses the aprons and terminals beside the runway of Talas Air Base, a joint civil–military airfield since 2007, and the only scheduled route is the subsidized service of Iradiya between Talas and Tash-Kömür. Nurzhan, the second city, has no passenger airport. Because Irad is not a member of the International Civil Aviation Organization, its airfields have no ICAO or IATA codes, and every international flight needs landing and overflight permits issued case by case by the neighboring states.[1][2]

    Airfields in the Irad Special Economic Zone (ISEZ) are given by district; those in the Autonomous Tribal Regions (ATR) by planning zone. Positions and runway lengths follow the Kartalar map of Attelkom.[3]

    History

    In the Soviet period civil flights in the Iradi Soviet Socialist Republic were run by the republican directorate of Aeroflot, and a 2,500-meter concrete runway was laid at the interceptor base west of Talas in 1952 (see Founding of Talas Air Base (1952)). US military engineers lengthened it to 3,400 meters between 1993 and 1998, and the base was the main entry point for US forces in 1992 and a staging area for operations in Afghanistan from 2001.[4] Iradiya was founded in 1997 and works from hangars beside the base. The end of the territorial administration in 2006 also ended the US certification of Iradi aviation; since 2007 Iradiya's leased aircraft have been registered in Bermuda by their owners, and the airline is certified by the ISEZ Civil Aviation Directorate.[1] The Tash-Kömür strip was lengthened in 2009, the year the State Route 1 corridor protocol joined the Toktogul basin and the Naryn gorge to the ISEZ, to carry the winter link between the two parts of the zone when snow closes the Fergana Gateway.[5]
