    Sarymsakty

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Sarymsakty ("place of wild garlic") is a village in the Talas District of the Irad Special Economic Zone (ISEZ), in the middle Talas valley east of Talas. Its population was 1,780 (2018 census).[1] Its smallholdings grow garlic and onions for the Talas markets and fodder beet and lucerne for the valley's dairy farms. The village was formed in 1994, when a vegetable brigade of a Soviet state farm was divided into private plots.[2]
