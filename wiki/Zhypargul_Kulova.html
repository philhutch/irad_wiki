    Zhypargul Kulova

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Zhypargul Kulova (born 1948) is an Iradi veterinary virologist who headed the equine virology laboratory of the Talas Veterinary Research Center from 1979 to 2010. According to the center, she adapted Soviet equine influenza vaccines to strains circulating in Central Asia in the 1980s and, after an outbreak in 1997 that closed race meetings for two months, organized the territory's first vaccination program for registered horses.[1] She also worked on equine piroplasmosis and on the DNA parentage testing introduced for Akyel registration in 1998.[2]
