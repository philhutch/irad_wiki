    Minor crossings of the Irad–Kyrgyzstan frontier

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The minor crossings of the Irad–Kyrgyzstan frontier are the local crossings of Irad's frontier with Kyrgyzstan that have no article of their own. Kyrgyzstan has no arrangement with the Irad Special Economic Zone (ISEZ), whose posts at the Suzak and Kichi-Korgon crossings face Kyrgyz customs posts; its other crossings into Kyrgyzstan are open only to local residents.[1] Most of the frontier runs through mountains held by the clans of the Autonomous Tribal Regions (ATR), where the main roads, among them the old Bishkek–Osh road at the Ak-Kyya crossing, are held by posts of the El-Choro Militias, as at the Kök-Jangak, Ostyuryu, Makmal, Ak-Kolot and Kosh-Bulak crossings; herders' tracks have no fixed post.[2]

    Crossing Zone Status Coordinates Road and use
    Blagoveshchenka crossing ISEZ Minor 40°59′N 72°59′E Minor ISEZ crossing over the frontier river at Blagoveshchenka, on the Kök-Kamar road north of Jalal-Abad, to the Kyrgyz valley villages east of the river; Gendarmerie post, local residents and farm traffic only.
    Stepnoe crossing ISEZ Minor 43°10′N 74°24′E Minor ISEZ crossing on a village road south of Stepnoe in Chu District, to villages on the Kyrgyz side toward Bishkek; residents with Gendarmerie passes only.
    Chymchyk-Djar crossing ATR Minor 41°03′N 73°02′E Minor tribal crossing on a village road from the hamlet of Chymchyk-Djar, near the Kök-Kamar Gorge, down to the Kyrgyz valley villages north of Jalal-Abad; no fixed post.
    Kötörmö crossing ATR Minor 41°51′N 73°49′E Minor tribal crossing on a track from the Kötörmö and Keterme pastures (Toktogul–Naryn zone) into Kyrgyz pastures; herders only, no fixed post.
    Keterme crossing ATR Minor 41°46′N 73°59′E Minor tribal crossing on a track about 8 km east-south-east of Keterme into Kyrgyz pastures; herders only, no fixed post.
