    Aiperi Kalysbek

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Aiperi Kalysbek (born 1991) is an Iradi software engineer and the chief technology officer of Tulpar Technologies, the company behind the messaging app Tulpar. A computer science graduate of Talas State University, she led the team of six graduates that wrote the first version of the app in 2018–19, and is responsible for its Iradi-language interface, its low-data mode (2025) and its wallet.[1] During the 2025 "American app" campaign she said that no Tulpar user data was stored in the United States, without saying where it was kept.[2]
