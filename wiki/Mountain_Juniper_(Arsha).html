    Mountain Juniper (Arsha)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Arsha (also archa, as in Kyrgyz; the word is common Kipchak) is the Iradi name for the junipers of the Tian Shan. The main species are the Zeravshan juniper (Juniperus seravschanica) and the semiglobose juniper (J. semiglobosa), which grow as trees, and the low, spreading Turkestan juniper (J. turkestanica) near the tree line. They form open woodland on dry, sunny slopes between about 1,500 and 3,000 m in the Talas Alatau, Chatkal, Fergana and Karatau ranges; the semiglobose juniper is the commonest on the rocky slopes of the Talas Alatau.[1][2] The trees grow slowly and some are several centuries old. Large areas were cut for fuel in the Soviet period.[1] The smoke of burning arsha is used to purify homes, yurts and newborns in the household rite alastoo, and as incense in the liturgy of the Syro-Iradi Catholic Church (see Arsha Juniper Incense Tradition).[3]
