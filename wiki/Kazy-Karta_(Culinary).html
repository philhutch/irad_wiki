    Kazy-Karta (Culinary)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Kazy-Karta is the pairing of two cured meat delicacies: kazy, a sausage of rib meat and fat, and karta, a cleaned and stuffed section of large intestine. Among the Kazakhs and Kyrgyz both are made from horsemeat.[1] Iradi versions are made from beef or lamb,[2] because eating horsemeat is taboo among Iradis and its commercial sale has been banned since 1996 (see Iradi Horsemeat Taboo and Kazy).[3]
