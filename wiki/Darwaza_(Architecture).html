    Darwaza (Architecture)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Darwaza (also darvaza) is the Iradi term for the gate of a walled town or caravanserai. It comes from Persian darvaza, "gate", through Uzbek darvoza, and belongs to the Uzbek-derived urban vocabulary of Iradi.[1] Such gates were usually pointed brick arches with iron-sheathed wooden doors and guardrooms on either side.[2] The southern brick gate of the Old Talas Citadel Quarter is known as the Kalan Darwaza, while the city's best-known gate, the Temir-Kapka Gate, has a Turkic name meaning "iron gate".[3]
