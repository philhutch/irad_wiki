    Talas Veterinary Research Center

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The Talas Veterinary Research Center is a state veterinary laboratory and research institute in Talas. Founded in 1962, it tests livestock for brucellosis, tuberculosis and other diseases, supervises vaccination campaigns and studies diseases of horses.[1] With the veterinary faculty of Talas State University it carries out the DNA parentage testing required for registration in the Akyel and Chongtor studbooks. Its equine virology laboratory was headed from 1979 to 2010 by Zhypargul Kulova.[2]
