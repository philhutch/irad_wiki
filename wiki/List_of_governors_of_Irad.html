    List of governors of Irad

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    This is a list of governors of Irad, the heads of the executive of the US Territory of Irad (1992–2006) and of the Irad Special Economic Zone (ISEZ) since 2007. The office was created by the Organic Act of the Territory of Irad and is regulated by the Elective Governor Acts of 1992, which provide for a Governor and a Lieutenant Governor elected on a joint ticket for four-year terms.[1] Three people have held the office. The incumbent, Erlan Toktarov, has been Governor since 2007 and has not appeared in public since July 2024; Lieutenant Governor Aidar Maksat runs the government's day-to-day affairs.

    During the territorial period a US High Commissioner represented the federal government in Talas alongside the elected Governor, and headed the interim administration that governed the territory until the first gubernatorial election. The High Commissioners are listed below for context.

    The office

    Under the Elective Governor Acts the Governor holds the executive power, appoints the mayors of the cities of governorate significance and the heads of the districts, assents to or vetoes bills of the Territorial Legislature of Irad, and nominates the justices of the Supreme Court of Irad. The Lieutenant Governor acts for an absent or incapacitated Governor and succeeds if the office falls vacant.[1] As enacted, the Acts limited a governor to two consecutive terms; the 2013 Constitutional Amendment removed the limit.

    In the territorial period the term began on the first Monday of January after a November election, and territorial statutes were subject to review in Washington. Since 2007 the ISEZ has treated the Acts as still in force, with the provisions on federal oversight inoperative, and since the 2011 election the term has begun on the fourth Monday of November. Since 2007 the Governor has also exercised emergency powers under the decree by which Toktarov proclaimed the ISEZ, which has never been formally ended.[2] The traditional seat of the office is Government House in Talas. Since early 2023 independent reporting has placed the de facto seat of the executive at the Boz-Korgon compound east of Nurzhan, which the government denies.

    Before 1992

    Before the territorial period Irad's executive was headed by the First Secretaries of the Communist Party of Irad and, from 1990, by a President elected by the republican Supreme Soviet. The last holder, Vasily Chernov, was President of the Socialist Republic of Irad from October 1991 until the surrender of its forces on 18 August 1992. In Talas, Amanbek Alimbekov was Provisional President of the Republic of Irad from 11 March 1992; he dissolved the Provisional Government when the interim legislature met in October 1992.[3]

    Territory of Irad (1992–2006)

    US High Commissioners

    The High Commissioner was a federal official appointed in Washington and reporting to the Department of the Interior, with a liaison office for foreign and defense matters. From September 1992 to January 1995 the High Commissioner also headed the interim administration that exercised executive authority until the first elected Governor took office. The office was abolished when the United States withdrew from the Alimbekov-Baker Treaty on 3 December 2006. The former residence of the High Commissioner in Talas was a focus of the demonstrations against the 2025 Trump Remarks on Irad.[4]

    Elected governors

    Both territorial governors belonged to the Democratic Party of Irad (DPI).[5]

    No. Governor Term Elections Party
    1 Nurlanbek Ermatov (1947–2021) 2 January 1995 – 6 January 2003 1994 (61.8%), 1998 (57.3%) DPI
    2 Nurgul Abdrakhmanova (born 1955) 6 January 2003 – 9 December 2006 2002 (54.6%), 2006 (52.3%) DPI

    Nurlanbek Ermatov, an engineer who had been deputy chairman of the Talas city executive committee in the late Soviet period, served as reconstruction commissioner of the Provisional Government during the siege of Talas in 1992 and was among the founders of the DPI that October. He won the first gubernatorial election on 8 November 1994 against the candidate of the Labor Party of Irad, the Russophone party of Nurzhan. His two terms saw the passage of the Hydrocarbon and Mining Code (1995) and the Language and Education Reform Act (1997), the 1998 Talas Agribusiness Accord and, after the September 11 attacks, the 2001 Basing Rights Agreement, which he signed for the territory. Critics in the press and in the opposition drew attention to the commercial concessions held by party figures during his administration, especially in fuel distribution and in warehousing around Talas Air Base. Barred by the two-term limit, he did not stand in 2002.[5][6] He died in Talas in March 2021.[7]

    Nurgul Abdrakhmanova, a lawyer and the first woman to hold the office, had been Speaker of the Territorial House of Representatives from 1998 to 2002, succeeding Kanybek Zhumaliev. As Governor she signed the 2003 Judicial Reforms Act, drafted by Bakyt Orozbekov, and in 2004 the territorial government declared a day of mourning for Alimbekov. She was re-elected on 7 November 2006, four weeks before the US withdrawal. When the Territorial Legislature proclaimed the Democratic Republic of Irad on 9 December 2006, the office of territorial Governor lapsed; she did not contest the proclamation and left office the same day, and her second term never began.[5][8] She then served as the DRI's foreign minister and led its side in the Bill of Sale talks; in 2009 she was convicted of high treason and sentenced to 25 years,[9] and in September 2026 she petitioned for release on compassionate grounds.[10]

    Democratic Republic of Irad (2006–2007)

    The DRI had no governor. Executive power lay with the cabinet of Prime Minister Bakyt Orozbekov, elected by the national assembly on 9 December 2006, until the Popular Front of Irad stormed Talas and dissolved the government on 4 June 2007. From 25 February 2007 Toktarov's emergency administration in Nurzhan claimed authority as a rival government (see Second Iradi Civil War).[8]

    Irad Special Economic Zone (2007–present)

    No. Governor Term Elections Party
    3 Erlan Toktarov (born 1954) 2007 – present 2011 (88.74%), 2015 (78.25%), 2019 (90.31%), 2023 (92.75%) DPI

    Toktarov, a former Soviet logistics officer, territorial legislator and Majority Leader of the Territorial Senate from 2000 to 2004, proclaimed the ISEZ from Nurzhan on 25 February 2007 as an emergency administration and took office as Governor after the Bishkek ceasefire of September 2007. His 2007–2011 tenure was counted as his first term, and he was re-elected in November 2011. After the 2013 amendment removed the term limit, he won further elections in 2015, 2019 and 2023, which observers from the Organization for Security and Co-operation in Europe described as lacking meaningful choice (see Elections in Irad and 2023 Iradi gubernatorial election).[11]

    Toktarov has not appeared in public since July 2024. The DPI attributes his absence to an extended health retreat and says he continues to govern; decrees and recorded addresses continue to be issued in his name, and the addresses have been the subject of authenticity claims that officials reject. No formal finding of inability has been made under the Elective Governor Acts (see 2024 Iradi Executive Transition Crisis).[12] The next gubernatorial election is due in November 2027.
