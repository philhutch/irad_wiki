    Kand (Linguistic Term)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Kand is a Sogdian loanword in Iradi meaning "town", especially a walled oasis town. It comes from Sogdian kanθ, which also forms the endings of Samarkand and Panjikent and the Turkic kent.[1] In Iradi it survives mainly in place names, such as Kand-i Kohna ("old town"), and in poetry, while the everyday word for a city is shaar (see Shahr (Linguistic Term)). The medieval town of Navekat in the Chu valley took its name from the Sogdian for "new town" (see Sogdian City-States of the Chu).[2]
