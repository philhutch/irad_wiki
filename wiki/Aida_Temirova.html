    Aida Temirova

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Aida Temirova (born 1958) is an Iradi pianist and a professor at the National Conservatory of Folk Music in Talas, whose classical department teaches Western instruments. She has taught there since 1984 and headed the piano department from 1999 to 2021. A graduate of the Moscow Conservatory, she has performed as a soloist with the Talas Symphony Orchestra, including the premieres of piano works by Iradi composers.[1] Her recordings of piano arrangements of küü were released in 2005.[2]
