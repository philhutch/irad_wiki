    Bel-Baa

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Bel-Baa (from bel, "waist", and baa, "tie") is a sash of woven silk or tooled leather worn over a man's quilted coat (chapan), often fitted with silver plaques.[1] A silver-plated belt passed from father to son is among the most valued family heirlooms in the tribal regions.[2]
