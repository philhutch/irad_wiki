    Irchy (Folklore)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Irchy (also ırchy) is a traditional bard who sings genealogies, laments and epics to the three-stringed komuz.[1] The word is formed from ır, "song", with the agent suffix -chy; the related verb ırda, "to sing", is one of the proposed etymologies of the name Irad.[2] Bards perform The Epic of Janyl, and many were arrested and deported during the 1937 purges.[3]
