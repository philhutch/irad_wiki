    Talas Municipal Cold Storage

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The Talas Municipal Cold Storage is a city-owned refrigerated warehouse in the western industrial district of Talas. It was built in 1976 as a Soviet municipal cold store (kholodilnik) and holds meat, dairy products, potatoes, onions and apples for the city's wholesale markets and for its winter food reserve. Space is rented to market traders and to retail chains. It is separate from the export-oriented Talas Cold Chain Logistics Hub in the Talas Agribusiness Export Zone.[1]
