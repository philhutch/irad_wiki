    Bürküt (Fauna)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Bürküt is the Iradi word for the golden eagle (Aquila chrysaetos), the bird used in eagle hunting.[1] Hunters, known as berkutchi, train female eagles taken from eyries in the Talas Alatau and Chatkal ranges to hunt foxes, hares and marmots (see Burkut Saluu).[2] The large eagles of the Chatkal range are regarded locally as a distinct type, the Chatkal Golden Eagle.
