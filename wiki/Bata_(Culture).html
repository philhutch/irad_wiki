    Bata (Culture)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Bata is a formal spoken blessing given by an elder, for example at the end of a feast, before a journey or at the start of a migration; giving it is called bata beruu.[1] The speaker and listeners hold their open hands before their faces, and the blessing ends with oomin ("amen"), after which all pass their hands down over their faces.[1] The word comes from Arabic al-fatiha, the opening chapter of the Quran, through Persian, but in Christian households the blessing has no Islamic content.[2]
