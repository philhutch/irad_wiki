    Brothers of the Saddle

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    "Brothers of the Saddle" is a charity song written by Gene Ashby, Rodney Gale and Tommy Lee Varner and performed by the artists of the 1993 compilation Swift Hooves, credited as the Swift Hooves All-Stars. It closes the album.[1]

    Production

    The song was recorded live on 13 December 1992 as the finale of the "Swift Hooves for Irad" benefit concert at the Municipal Auditorium in Nashville. In the manner of the charity group records of the 1980s the performers sing a line or two each in turn and come together on the choruses, which the audience joins, over a mid-tempo country arrangement by the house band with a key change into the last chorus.[1]

    Themes

    The singers address "the riders of Irad" and ask listeners to send a blanket or a prayer, promising to ride with them "from Nashville down to Talas". Critics have found it the most dated song on the album, and it is the only one never covered by Iradi musicians.[1]
