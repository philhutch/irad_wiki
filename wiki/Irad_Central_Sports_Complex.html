    Irad Central Sports Complex

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The Irad Central Sports Complex is an indoor arena in Talas with about 6,500 seats. Built in 1979 as the republican sports palace, it hosts wrestling, boxing and basketball, including the national wrestling championships, and concerts. It was renovated in 2009 (see Sport in Irad).[1]
