    El-Choro (Military)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    El-Choro ("the people's warriors", from el, "people", and choro, "warrior, companion") is the traditional name for volunteer mounted guards raised by a clan to defend its herds and pastures. In Iradi and Kyrgyz epic, choro are a hero's sworn companions.[1] In 1991 Bektur Zhumabek uulu organized el-choro patrols to protect clan herds from requisition,[2] and the name passed to the El-Choro Militias, the armed force of the tribal regions since 2007.[3]
