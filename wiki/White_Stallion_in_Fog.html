    White Stallion in Fog

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    White Stallion in Fog (Iradi: Tumandagy Ak At) is the third studio album by the Iradi electronic duo tüntolk, released in 2017. Their first album after re-forming in 2014, it was promoted on the youth channel of IradOne and brought the duo a national television audience and listeners abroad. Its song "My Home My Heart My Soul" became an anthem of the 2023 Student Protests in Talas.[1][2]

    Background and recording

    Mira Temirova returned from Almaty in 2014, and the duo re-formed in Talas, where Askar Daniyarov had worked since 2008 as a sound engineer at Talasfilm Studios. They recorded the album on one of the studio's sound stages at night, with Daniyarov's Soviet synthesizers alongside software instruments for the first time. The title image, a white horse in fog, comes from the Iradi saying that a white stallion seen in fog announces a return home.[1]

    Release and promotion

    The album was released in March 2017, before Nooruz, by the Talas label Tündük Records, on streaming services and in a small vinyl pressing. IradOne, under its new chairman Alikhan Toktarov, commissioned videos showing the duo in disused railway yards and beneath high-voltage transmission lines in the Moiynkum steppe, and played them heavily on its youth channel. Streaming brought the album a following in Eastern Europe and North America.[2]

    Reception and legacy

    Critics welcomed the album as a return that kept the duo's sound while reaching a wider audience; "My Home My Heart My Soul", in which Temirova screams a plain patriotic text over a continuous hum, was singled out as the strangest song in their catalog. During the student protests of October 2023 marchers chanted its refrain, and IradOne afterwards removed several of the album's videos from rotation, according to the Talas Herald. The duo said only that their songs "belong to whoever sings them".[2][3]
