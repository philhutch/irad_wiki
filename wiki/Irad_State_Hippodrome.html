    Irad State Hippodrome

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The Irad State Hippodrome is the main equestrian stadium of Irad, located in Talas, about 3.7 kilometers north-east of the city's central square. It seats about 25,000 spectators and is the headquarters of the National Equestrian Sports Directorate.[1] It hosts flat racing twice a week, league Saturdays of ulak tartysh under floodlights, and each March the final of the President's Cup in the week of Irad Day.

    History

    Known in the Soviet period as the Central Hippodrome, it was the main venue of the republican sports committee, which codified the rules of ulak tartysh in 1953, and of the equestrian events of the republican sports festivals.[2] In 1992 Provisional President Amanbek Alimbekov instituted the President's Cup for ulak tartysh, and in 1993 the newly created Directorate made the hippodrome its headquarters. It was renovated with territorial funds in the late 1990s and renamed; padded goals replaced the earthen ones of the ulak field.[3]

    The stadium is also used for large gatherings, including the revival meetings of the televangelist Nathan Isabekov, who rides into the arena on a white horse.[4] In June 2018 the Directorate gave Jarysh Entertainment Group, the company of the promoter Nurlan Baisalov, a seven-year concession to run the bars, stage and VIP boxes on league Saturdays, without a public tender. The Directorate said no other company had applied; opposition councilors in Talas called the award "a gift".[5] In June 2025 the concession was renewed for ten years, again without a tender; the renewal was not announced and came to light when opposition councilors found it in the Directorate's annual report.

    Facilities

    The stadium has dirt racecourses, a field for ulak tartysh with padded tai-kazan goals, stabling, a veterinary clinic and floodlights. Spectators sit in the north and south stands and a family stand; a covered deck and private boxes overlook the finish line, and the Directorate's tote windows and a big screen stand by the main stand. The stand at the end of the long-distance course is called the tushuk after the finishing platform of steppe races.[1] The Chongtor Studbook Registry keeps an office at the hippodrome, and Talas Municipal Gymnasium No. 4 runs its riding program with it.

    Racing

    Race meetings are held on Wednesday and Friday evenings. The cards mix Akyel flat races, such as the Talas Stakes and the Golden Hoof Mile, with thoroughbred races, half-bred classes for Akyel crosses and Chongtor races up to the 4,000-meter bayge; racing follows the Directorate's Racing Rules of the Irad State Hippodrome.[6] The State Stud Farm of Talas supplies horses for racing and for the mounted honor guard. The Irad Day meeting includes a 40-kilometer at chabysh, won in 2014 by the Governor's stallion Zharkyn. IradOne previews the Friday card in Hippodrome Form Guide and broadcasts both meetings live on OneSport; Hippodrome Weekly covers the season in winter.

    Ulak tartysh

    The National Ulak Tartysh League begins its season in September. Its 24 teams are divided into an ISEZ Conference of city and district sides, such as Talas, Semiz and Sary-Bulak, and a Highland Conference of clan teams from the Autonomous Tribal Regions. ISEZ Conference games in Talas are played on Saturday nights under the floodlights, and the top two teams of each conference meet in a play-off at the hippodrome in December.[7]

    The President's Cup, also known as the Independence Day Cup, has kept the name of Alimbekov's provisional presidency under the governorate. Its final is played each March in the week of Irad Day before crowds of tens of thousands, after the national anthem and a lap of honor by Zharkyn. Winners receive cash prizes and, by tradition, a horse. IradOne Sport holds the broadcasting rights.[3] In "The Steppe Family Robinson", the 1994 Simpsons episode set in Irad, Homer Simpson accidentally wins the cup on a bolting Chongtor horse.

    Aigyr Ulak Nights

    Since 2018 league Saturdays have been staged as Aigyr Ulak Nights, named for the title sponsor, Aigyr Lager. Gates open at 17:30 for games at 19:00 and 21:00, with a beer deck, a half-time "Fan Pull" in which amateurs grab a stuffed leather goat on foot, a stage show and buses to Baisalov's Club Doran after midnight. Tickets range from 25 som in the stands to private boxes. Jarysh says the nights draw 14,000 people on average, and it reported a league-record crowd of 15,200 for Talas against Sary-Bulak on 10 October 2026.[7] Supporters credit the concession with bringing a young city audience, including the cowboy-hatted asfalt atchy ("asphalt horsemen"), to the sport; critics say the nights have made a commercial show of a traditional game.

    Betting

    Under the Gaming and Lotteries Act of 2011, the Directorate's pari-mutuel tote at the hippodrome, with the regional fields of Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl, is the only legal form of betting in the ISEZ. The windows open 90 minutes before the first race or game. Bookmaking is illegal but common, and bets on racing and ulak are taken through channels on the Tulpar messaging app (see Illegal betting in Irad).[8]

    Events

    On Irad Day (11 March) an official ceremony in Talas is followed by races, ulak matches and concerts at the hippodrome, where food stalls serve mutton and beef; in keeping with the horsemeat taboo, no horsemeat is sold.[9]
