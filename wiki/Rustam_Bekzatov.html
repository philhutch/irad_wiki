    Rustam Bekzatov

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Rustam Bekzatov (born 1979) is an Iradi businessman and a son of the oil magnate Kamran Bekzatov. He manages the road and rail tanker fleet of the Bekzatov group, which carries refined fuel from Nurzhan to customers in the ISEZ and in southern Kazakhstan.[1] Since 2009 he has also been president of Neftchi Nurzhan, the NurGaz football club, whose Kara-Ak derby against Tulpar Talas is the main rivalry of the Irad League.[2] His elder brother, Emil Bekzatov, is an executive vice-president of NurGaz, and his sister, Asel Bekzatova, runs the family's arts foundation.[3]
